Built on the job, not in a boardroom.

Blackbeam is a Springfield, MO construction company with a simple idea: a small crew, backed by the right automation, can run like a company ten times its size — and treat every client like it.

01Who we are

A crew that builds — and a system that runs the business.

We started Blackbeam on the ground in Springfield, doing the work ourselves. Along the way we got tired of the same thing everyone in construction complains about: slow quotes, missed calls, and paperwork that piles up while the real work waits.

So we built our own back office to fix it — and ran it on our own jobs first. The result is a crew that answers in minutes, quotes the same day, and never loses track of a project. Same badge on the jackets, the trucks, and the software.

02Vision & mission

Every small crew in America, running like a company ten times its size.

That's the vision. The mission is how we get there: prove the self-running back office on our own jobs first — then put it under every contractor who'd rather build than do paperwork.

Nothing we offer is theory. If it hasn't earned its place on a real Blackbeam job, it doesn't ship.

03How we operate

The way we work is the way we talk.
